Concrete Glass

Max Bill's concrete geometry and Apple's Liquid Glass want opposite things — one matte, orthogonal and printed; the other fluid, translucent and lensing. Rather than average them into mush, this study gives each one a layer of its own.

The page is Bill

Content stays opaque, matte and orthogonal. Square corners, hairline rules, flat colour fields with no gradient, a 1.25 type scale, and tick instruments lifted from his Junghans dials. No pictograms — every row names itself in words, so nothing needs decoding. Nothing in the data layer is translucent either; measurements are printed matter.

The lens is Apple

Only the floating control layer is glass: the header, the detached tab-bar capsule, switches and buttons. Each one blurs, saturates and throws a specular rim, refracting the geometry that scrolls underneath. Controls hover; they never sit down on the page.

The split is the whole idea. Refraction only reads as refraction when there is something precise behind it to bend — blur a soft surface and you see nothing at all.

Scroll any screen — the glass refracts the content passing beneath it.

the word for the thing, then its contextvalue

No pictograms anywhere a row names something. A row identifies itself with the word, sets context in mono beneath it, and puts the number on the right rail where the eye already waits. An abstract mark that needs a legend is slower to read than the label it replaced — so the label stays and the mark goes.

Scale · ratio 1.25

4629 2318.5 1512 9.5

Archivo at 200–600 for everything read as language, IBM Plex Mono for every label, unit and timestamp — instrument silkscreen, never prose. Each step is the last × 1.25, rounded to the half-point. Bill sized by rule, not by eye.

Concrete fields

InkSlateSkyOchreRustPaper

Your teal and rust survive intact. Ochre, slate and sky join as Konkrete Kunst colour fields — flat, unmixed, and only ever used to distinguish one signal from another. Never a gradient in the content layer.

Glass spec

blur 26 · saturate 185%, a 1.1 px specular rim top and bottom, and a soft cast shadow. Applied to four things only: header, tab bar, switches, round buttons. Anything holding a number stays opaque.
